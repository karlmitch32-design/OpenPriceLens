How OpenPriceLens works

OpenPriceLens is being built as a free, independent UK price-comparison project. It does not currently earn commission or accept payments.

Real offers only

Prices will only be shown when they come from a source we are permitted to use. We will not invent prices or pretend an estimate is a live offer.

Show when a price was checked

Every offer should include the retailer, product condition, and last-checked time when the source provides that information. Prices and stock can change, so confirm them with the retailer.

Links should be clear

Where permitted, offer links will take you to the retailer's product page. External Google or Bing results are separate and are not verified OpenPriceLens offers.

No account needed for this starter page

Product searches and photo previews run in your browser. A chosen photo is not uploaded by this page, and photo recognition is not connected yet.

We are still checking which product-data sources allow this use. Until that is confirmed, the verified-offer catalogue may be empty.

OpenPriceLens is an independent project and is not affiliated with any retailer. This starter page does not scrape retailer websites or claim to provide live offers. Never add private API keys to this public website.
